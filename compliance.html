Kids Monitor trust and compliance

How monitoring works, which permissions are required, what plan features are available, and where to find the governing policies.

Purpose of the service

Kids Monitor is a consent-based family safety aid. It provides live or last-known location, safety alerts, SOS, optional location history, Location Logs and Safe Areas.

  • Monitoring is visible on the child device.
  • The child can stop monitoring.
  • Parent or legal-guardian authority is required.
  • The service is not an emergency-response provider.

Required and optional permissions

  • Location: foreground and, where supported, background location for live status, route history and Safe Area evaluation.
  • Notifications: parent safety alerts and the visible child monitoring status where required.
  • Camera: only when the user chooses to scan a pairing QR code.
  • Battery optimization exemption: optional Android setting that helps location and safety monitoring continue while the device is idle.

Safety and data controls

  • Parents can remove a linked parent or leave the shared family.
  • The last guardian’s departure stops monitoring and queues child-data cleanup.
  • Guardian invitations are one-use, expire after two hours and are revoked when membership changes.
  • Free keeps current/last-known location, one Safe Area and recent safety alerts without retaining route history.
  • Paid plans add route history, Location Logs and up to 95 Safe Areas according to the plan.
  • SOS is available on every plan.
  • Permanent-Free Parent Home may show one small non-personalized, general-audience banner after the trial. Child mode, SOS, alerts, checkout, policy screens, the trial and paid plans remain ad-free.
  • Child location and child activity are not used for advertising or profiling.
  • Kids Adventure games, painting, progress and saved pictures work offline and are stored only in app-private storage on the child device.
  • Child artwork is not uploaded to Firebase, a parent account or an AI provider and can be deleted from My Art.

Service limitations

Location, notifications and alerts can be delayed or unavailable because of GPS accuracy, permissions, battery management, network connectivity, Firebase services, Google Maps, push delivery, device settings or service outages.

Do not rely on Kids Monitor as the only method of emergency response. Contact appropriate local emergency services when a person is in immediate danger.

Responsible use

  • Use only with clear authority and required consent.
  • Follow local child-safety, privacy, employment and data-protection laws.
  • Do not use the service for covert surveillance of an adult or non-consenting person.
  • Protect account credentials, guardian codes and parent devices.
