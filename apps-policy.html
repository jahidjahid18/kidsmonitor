Kids Monitor Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 17, 2026

Kids Monitor ("we", "our", "the app") is a family safety application with parent and child companion experiences. This Privacy Policy explains what information we process, why we process it, how it is protected, and the controls available to parents and guardians.

Who Can Use the App

  • Parent accounts must be created and controlled by an adult parent or legal guardian.
  • A child device must be paired only with permission and legal authority under the laws that apply in the relevant location.
  • The Service is not directed to children under 13 for independent account creation.

Information We Process

  • Account information such as name, email address, profile photo, and authentication identifiers.
  • Device and pairing information needed to link parent and child devices securely.
  • Location information, including current or last-known coordinates, movement history, location status, and Safe Area events.
  • Safety events such as SOS alerts, geofence entry or exit events, permission-health updates, low-battery events, and offline status.
  • Voice messages, if the voice-message feature is used by a linked device.
  • Subscription and purchase status processed through Google Play and RevenueCat.
  • Family-relationship records showing the primary guardian, additional guardian, shared children, subscription payer, covered guardian status, plan source, expiry, guardian removals and related audit records. A shared child may have at most two linked parent accounts. Multiple phones signed in to the same parent account do not create additional parent accounts.
  • Administrative usage counters, support notes and security-audit records used to operate plan limits and investigate support issues.
  • Technical logs, diagnostics, crash information, and security records used to operate and protect the Service.

Permissions

Depending on enabled features, the app may request:

  • Foreground and background location permission for live location, movement history, and Safe Areas.
  • Notification permission for alerts and ongoing status.
  • Camera permission only when scanning a pairing QR code.
  • Battery-optimization exemption so location and protection features continue while the device is idle.

A paired child device displays an ongoing notification while monitoring is active and permits the child to stop monitoring.

How We Use Information

  • To provide live location, history, SOS, and Safe Area features.
  • To deliver safety alerts configured by a parent or guardian.
  • To manage subscriptions and plan-based feature limits.
  • To provide authorized support for guardian relationships, subscription ownership and damaged or orphaned child records.
  • To secure accounts, prevent abuse, troubleshoot issues, and improve reliability.
  • To comply with applicable legal and platform obligations.

Data Sharing

Kids Monitor does not sell personal data.

Information may be processed by service providers required to operate the app, including:

  • Firebase services for authentication, database, storage, messaging, analytics, crash reporting, App Check, and backend functions.
  • Google Maps for map display and location search.
  • Google Play Billing and RevenueCat for purchases and subscriptions.
  • Google AdMob for the parent-only, non-personalized permanent-Free banner and regional advertising consent choices.

We may also disclose information when required by law, necessary to protect safety or legal rights, or as part of a lawful organizational transaction.

Advertising

The parent experience may show a small, non-personalized banner to permanent-Free accounts after the trial ends. Ads are not shown in Child mode, during the trial, on paid plans, or on SOS, alert, subscription checkout and policy screens. The app does not use child location, child activity, or child profile data for advertising and does not sell personal data.

Data Retention

  • Free: current or last-known location, no route-history retention, and approximately 24 hours of recent alert history.
  • Live Essentials: up to 3 days of location history.
  • Family Start: up to 7 days of location history.
  • Family: up to 14 days of location history.
  • Family Plus and Family Max: up to 30 days of location history.
  • Account and pairing information is retained while the account is active or until deletion is requested.
  • Backups, security records, and financial records may be retained for the period required by applicable law or legitimate operational needs.

Parents can delete their account and account-linked data in the app or through the account-deletion page.

Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational safeguards, including authenticated access, server-side authorization checks, Firebase security rules, App Check, and restricted administrative access. Authorized administrators can view account, subscription and linked child metadata when needed for support. Administrative changes are subject to access controls and audit records. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so users should protect their devices, credentials, and pairing codes.

Children and Family Safety

  • Parents and guardians are responsible for lawful use and consent.
  • Monitoring must not be used for covert surveillance of adults or non-consenting persons.
  • A child device is visibly paired, shows an ongoing monitoring notification where required, and can stop monitoring.
  • The Service is a safety aid, not an emergency-response service or guaranteed personal-safety system.

Your Controls

  • Parents can manage linked children and alerts on every plan. Safe Areas include one place on Free and up to 95 when either linked parent has an active paid plan. Route history is paid-only.
  • Either linked parent can leave a shared family without deleting the child, and either linked parent can remove the other parent. If the primary guardian leaves while another guardian remains, ownership transfers to the remaining guardian. The removed parent immediately loses future access to the child and any family-plan benefit shared through that child. The remaining guardians' effective plan is recalculated immediately.
  • When guardian membership changes, unused invitations for that child are revoked. A removed parent must receive a new invitation and can rejoin only if a parent slot is available. A child may have at most two linked parent accounts.
  • Records already stored in the removed parent's account, such as previous alert entries, audit records, or messages, may remain for the applicable retention period or until that account and its data are deleted.
  • The child and its monitoring data are permanently deleted only when the last guardian removes the child or when authorized support performs an audited deletion to resolve a verified account issue.
  • Users can revoke device permissions through system settings.
  • Parents can remove linked devices and delete their account.
  • Subscriptions can be managed or cancelled through Google Play.

Policy Updates

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The latest version will be published on this page with an updated date. Material changes will be communicated through appropriate app or account channels where required.
