Three-day Live Essentials trial

A new parent receives one Live Essentials trial after the first successful child pairing. The trial is intended to demonstrate the paid experience before a purchase decision.

  • 3 days of Live Essentials features for one child
  • 5-minute default updates (1-minute minimum); faster options remain locked during the trial
  • 3-day route history and Location Logs
  • Up to 95 Safe Areas and unlimited map access
  • No automatic subscription conversion
  • Returns to permanent Free unless a plan is purchased
  • One server-controlled trial per parent account
  • No duplicate trial when the parent already has paid or complimentary access
  • After the trial: Free plan with 15-minute updates, 1 Safe Area and ads
  • Google Play cannot sell Live Essentials during its own trial; upgrade to a higher plan or subscribe after it ends
The trial starts after child pairing, not at account creation, so setup time does not consume the three days.

How family sharing works

  • A shared child can have at most two linked parent accounts.
  • The primary parent and one additional guardian both see the same child.
  • The highest active plan from either linked parent applies to that shared child.
  • One active paid plan is enough for shared-child benefits.
  • Linking does not merge, cancel, refund or prorate separate subscriptions.
  • Each parent keeps their own child-creation capacity.
  • Either parent can leave or remove the other parent.
  • If the primary parent leaves, the remaining parent becomes primary.
  • If the last parent leaves, child monitoring stops and cleanup is queued.
Guardian codes are one-use, expire after two hours, and are revoked when the child’s parent membership changes.

Child capacity is not pooled

Each parent has an independent number of child slots. A shared child counts once in each linked parent’s account.

Family Start + Family

The Family Start parent can create up to 2 children. The Family parent can create up to 3. They do not receive a combined five shared slots. If every child is shared by both parents, the shared total is limited by the lower parent’s capacity.

Free parent linked to a paid parent

The Free parent receives shared-child plan features, but remains limited to one child in their own account. The paid plan does not transfer child-creation capacity.

Existing children remain visible if a plan later changes, but adding another child is blocked when the parent is at their own capacity.

Safe Areas

  • Free includes one Safe Area for the child.
  • Live Essentials and all higher plans allow up to 95 Safe Areas.
  • If either linked parent has an active paid plan, the shared child gets the paid Safe Area allowance.
  • Safe Areas generate arrival and departure alerts using GPS hysteresis.
  • Both linked parents can receive the alert.
  • Safe Areas do not consume child-creation or map-open allowance.
  • When a paid plan returns to Free, one active area remains and extra areas are paused.
  • Restoring paid access reactivates only areas paused by the plan downgrade; areas manually disabled by a parent stay disabled.

Safety alerts and SOS

  • SOS is available on every plan and is not a paid-only feature.
  • One SOS alert is created for each linked parent.
  • Every active parent-device notification token is contacted.
  • The child’s last-known location is sent immediately, followed by an attempt to obtain a fresher location.
  • SOS map images are retained for 24 hours.
  • Low-battery, offline and permission/device-health alerts remain available on Free.
  • Free alert history is limited to recent alerts, approximately 24 hours.
  • Paid alert and location history follow the purchased retention window.

Child experience and Kids Adventure

Child mode keeps monitoring visible while making the map compact and optional to expand.

  • Monitoring status, parent connection, last update and SOS remain visible.
  • The map starts as a compact card and can expand when needed.
  • Collapsing the map does not stop location sharing.
  • Kids Adventure includes offline memory, pattern, number, sorting, story, word, animal and hands-on learning games.
  • Matching Puzzle Park adds 100 local matching activities: 15 tap-match, 25 finger-jigsaw, 25 drag-and-match, 20 finger-trace mazes and 15 rotating-wheel puzzles.
  • Art Studio provides finger painting with eight colours, three brush sizes, undo and clear controls.
  • Coloring Pals includes local animal pages with 20 scene backgrounds and the same painting controls.
  • A calm breathing activity is available at any time.
  • Every activity works without internet access, an AI service, a game purchase or additional payment.
  • Activities contain no ads, chat, leaderboards or public sharing.
  • Finished-activity stars and saved pictures stay in private per-child storage on the device.
  • My Art lets the child view and delete locally saved pictures.
  • Pictures are not uploaded to Firebase, a parent account or any AI service.
  • SOS remains available from the Adventure screen.

Advertising

The parent experience may show one small non-personalized banner for permanent-Free accounts after the three-day trial. The child experience may show one small, child-directed, non-personalized, general-audience banner below the content.

  • No banner during the parent trial or on paid parent plans
  • Child banner is child-directed, G-rated and never personalized
  • No pop-ups or full-screen ads
  • No ads on SOS, alerts, checkout, pairing, permission or policy screens
  • No child location or activity used for advertising or profiling
  • General-audience content rating and non-personalized requests only
  • Regional consent choices are available where required

Upgrades, cancellation and downgrades

  • Purchases, renewals, cancellations and refunds are handled through Google Play.
  • Cancelling normally keeps paid access active until the verified expiry date.
  • When paid access expires, the highest remaining active linked plan applies.
  • If no linked parent has paid access, the child returns to Free limits.
  • Route history outside the new retention window may be deleted by scheduled cleanup.
  • Extra Safe Areas are paused when the child returns to the one-area Free allowance.
  • Linking accounts never automatically cancels either subscription.
  • Revenue is attributed only to the actual subscription payer; covered parents are not charged by Kids Monitor.
To avoid paying twice for the same shared-child benefits, confirm the other parent’s plan is active before cancelling your own. Manage cancellation through your Google Play subscriptions.

Subscription FAQ

Do both parents need a paid plan?

No. One active paid plan is enough for the shared child. A second paid plan is only useful for that parent’s own child-creation capacity or independent coverage.

Are the two parents’ child limits added together?

No. Each parent’s capacity remains independent. Family Start + Family does not mean both parents can create five children. The shared child counts against both accounts.

If one parent has Family and the other is Free, what does the Free parent get?

The Free parent receives Family benefits for the shared child, including the 5-minute paid default refresh, 14-day history, Location Logs and up to 95 Safe Areas. Either linked parent can change that child's refresh time because the shared child has an active paid plan. The Free parent still has a one-child creation limit.

Does removing a parent cancel their Google Play subscription?

No. Removing the family link does not cancel billing. The account owner must manage or cancel the subscription through Google Play.

Does SOS require a paid plan?

No. SOS is available on Free and every paid plan.

Is a map screen a paid Google Maps charge?

No. Android Maps SDK usage is currently provided without a per-map-load charge under the published Maps SDK pricing. Backend Firestore reads may still be consumed when history or current locations are loaded.

What happens after a plan expires?

The app checks the highest remaining active linked plan. If no plan remains, updates return to 15 minutes, route history and Logs stop being available, and the child returns to one Safe Area.
